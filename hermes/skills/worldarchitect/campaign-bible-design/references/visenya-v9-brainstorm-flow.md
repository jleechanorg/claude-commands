## Pre-brainstorm context

User's original ask (verbatim, 1 message):
> "Look at the Google Docs and /wiki-search my Visenya campaigns and brainstorm a way to make them more interesting or better for what I like and use Google cli and then design a Visenya v9 campaign and let's set it during the time of dunk and egg and make Visenya known as the blood dragon title and a level 6 assassin gloomstalker custom class"

Brainstorm protocol triggered: multiple-choice questions one at a time, hard-gate no-implementation-before-design-approval.

---

## Q1 — Adversary type + worldbuilding aftermath endgame shape

**Question:** "How should the L20+ endgame shape work? Adversarial match + worldbuilding aftermath (B+C), single mortal anchor focus, or something else?"

**User's pick:** **B and C** — adversarial match + worldbuilding aftermath. The endgame is a fight *plus* the world-state aftermath. Single mortal-anchor focus was rejected (too narrow).

---

## Q2 — Eastern origin + Doom framing

**User's input (verbatim):**
> "First song is fine but make it fit the campaign and lore and maybe we can do someone from an eastern country like YI TI golden empire or shadowlands like east of Valyria and let's make the Valyrian doom apotheosis thing again this campaign"

**Three constraints locked:**
1. **First Song as the central adversary** (the major campaign antagonist)
2. **Eastern origin** — Yi Ti Golden Empire, Asshai, or Shadow Lands east of Valyria
3. **Valyrian Doom = APOTHEOSIS, not extinction** — the Freehold transcended via the Fourteen Flames forge

---

## Q3 — Endgame resolution shape

**Question:** "What resolution shape should the campaign support? Joining (apotheosis-as-homecoming), Replacement (the kill), Refusal (the unprecedented choice), or open-ended?"

**User's pushback (verbatim):**
> "Don't make me pick an ending just save possibilities we are designing the campaign not a fully decided story"

**Decision locked:** The campaign is a **system, not a story**. The player picks the ending at the table. The bible must ship with multiple documented endings + a player-defined slot.

This is the canonical "campaign ≠ story" correction. Future sessions must not propose canonical endings mid-brainstorm.

---

## Q4 — Choice shape (rephrased after Q3 correction)

**Question:** "What kind of choice should the player get at L20+? Three canonical branches + open-ended freeform, or one system with multiple emergent endings?"

**User's pick:** **B) One system, multiple emergent endings.** The bible ships with the *system* and lets the *story* emerge from play. The player has more freedom but the design is still coherent.

---

## Q5 — Sanguine Thread mechanic shape

**User's pushback (verbatim):**
> "What is Sanguine Thread? I think that might be too much god of murder? i remember i said to take inspiration from that bhaal campaign and dont directly copy"

**Correction locked:** The Sanguine Thread as previously proposed (book of names → 7-tier book transformation) was too close to PR #8483's Bhaal module. **Reframe required:** The Sanguine Thread should be about Visenya's *lineage*, not about *sin*. The "thread" is her bloodline, not a record of kills.

This is the canonical "take inspiration, don't copy" correction. Future sessions must apply the copy-vs-inspire test before shipping mechanics.

---

## Q6 — Eastern-framing reconciliation

**Question:** "How do we reconcile the eastern-framing (Yi Ti / Asshai / Shadow Lands) with the Doom-as-apotheosis framing? The First Song lives in the Shadow Lands, the Doom's basalt is a portal; she's split between both; or she lives only in the basalt?"

**Status:** held for user pick — eventually combined with Q8 into the First Song identity decision.

## Q7 — Final design shape

**User's input (verbatim):**
> "I want level 20+ god campaign and divine ranks. Reputation is cool and maybe it makes her stronger and more divine? Like in tyranny the archons. One system multiple emergent endings. For first song lets make it one of the stronger previous visenya campaigns like V1-V8"

**Four constraints locked:**
1. **L20+ is a real god-tier arc with Divine Ranks** (Tyranny-of-Dragons Archon tier shape)
2. **Reputation Die → Divine Rank scaling** — the Blood Dragon's legend is the *engine* of her ascension
3. **One system, multiple emergent endings** (locked from Q4)
4. **First Song = one of the stronger prior Visenya campaigns (V1-V8)** — a specific version who won, became divine, turned sadistic

**Question:** "Which prior Visenya is the First Song? V6-Visenya (Blood Dragon who got bored), V8-Visenya (Sovereign Shadow who built a state without kings), V2-Visenya (Belaerys Apex Heir who triggered the Doom)?"

**User's pick:** **A) V6-Visenya.**

## Final design pivots (7 locked decisions)

1. **Doom = apotheosis, not extinction.** The Freehold didn't fall; it *transcended*. The Doom was the *forge*.
2. **First Song = V6-Visenya as an interdimensional exile.** Performed the Doom ritual, became a god, turned everyone in her world into "playthings" — but didn't realize the cost was *zombie-souls*. Her world is a stage of puppets with no players.
3. **First Song uses the same god mechanics Visenya has** — identical Stress-Line Sight, Sanguine Thread, Reputation Die, Divine Rank. The Magic Barrier System is the *only* difference.
4. **Sanguine Thread = lineage, not sin.** The Book of the Blood Dragon transforms at each tier (Red Ledger → Wound Ledger → Book of Names → Tapestry → Mantle of the Sanguine Slayer → Mantle of the Radiant Slayer → Thread Eternal).
5. **Reputation Die → Divine Rank coupling.** The Blood Dragon's legend is the *primary engine* of her divine ascension.
6. **L20+ god campaign with Archon tier** (Tyranny-of-Dragons shape, not copy). 7 tiers.
7. **Four emergent endings** (player choice at the table, no canonical resolution): Joining / Replacement / Refusal / Player-defined.

## Ship sequence (reusable)

1. Brainstorm Q1-Q8 with status checks at each step
2. Write spec to `/tmp/<campaign>.md` (with markdown headings + tables)
3. Create Google Doc (`gog docs create`) + write content (`gog docs write --replace --markdown --file <spec>`)
4. Create llm-wiki source page + concept pages (one per major mechanic) + entity pages (one per setting element)
5. Update llm-wiki `wiki/index.md` + `wiki/log.md`
6. Commit + push to `jleechanorg/llm-wiki` `origin/main`
7. Verify all wiki files return 200 on `raw.githubusercontent.com`
8. Create fresh worktree from `origin/main` of `$GITHUB_REPOSITORY`
9. Write `world_reference/campaign_module_<short>.md` using the established naming convention
10. Commit + push + open PR
11. Drive to merge: GATE-1 self-referential FAIL + GATE-3 CodeRabbit rate-limit are non-blocking on `world_reference/` content PRs — `gh pr merge --merge --delete-branch` works directly (branch protection on main has no required checks)
12. Update llm-wiki `wiki/log.md` with ship entry
13. Post completion summary to original Slack thread
